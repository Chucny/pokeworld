## 🚀 Overview

**Pokeworld** is a next-generation, browser-based Augmented Reality (AR) game inspired by Pokémon GO. Unlike traditional games that rely on a digital map, Pokeworld immerses you directly into the action. Turn your real-world surroundings into a living, breathing Pokémon habitat and start your journey to become a legendary Trainer today!

---

## ✨ Features

### 📸 Pure AR Catching Mechanics
Forget staring at a virtual map. Pokeworld features a **pure AR experience** where Pokémon spawn directly into your real-world environment. Look around through your camera, find them, and catch them!

### 🎒 Live Trainer Inventory & Storage
Keep track of your journey with a fully functional **Pokémon Storage** system. Inspect your caught Pokémon, view their unique stats (including weight, height, and specific attributes), or transfer them to optimize your inventory.

### ☁️ Persistent Accounts
Your progress is safe. Save your caught Pokémon, trainer stats, and achievements by creating a **Pokeworld Account**. Log in from any device and pick up right where you left off.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Visit the Game:** Head over to [Pokeworld](https://chucny.github.io/pokeworld).
2. **Create an Account:** Sign up or log in to ensure your progress is saved.
3. **Grant Camera Permissions:** Enable AR mode to let Pokémon spawn
4. **Catch 'Em All:** Catch Pokemon in pure AR mode! There's no map, just pure exploring and fun!

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

This project is built using modern web technologies to deliver a seamless AR experience directly in the browser without any extra downloads:

*   **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, Vanilla JavaScript
*   **AR Engine:** A 3DOF AR engine coded in Vanilla JavaScript from scratch!
*   **Hosting:** GitHub Pages
